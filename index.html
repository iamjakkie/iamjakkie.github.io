§2 · OPERATOR

Short version.

I build navigation for drones that can't trust GPS. I'm also doing a PhD on it, which is the same problem with more paperwork and fewer propellers.

Mostly Rust, because I like my bugs caught at compile time and my segfaults theoretical. I write the maths by hand until it stops lying to me. Before this: years of data infrastructure, which is why I still think every problem is a time series. I'm usually right.

Nothing here is a product page. Things get shown when they work. Not when they compile. When they work.
